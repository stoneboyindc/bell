Awakening Bell

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2026

This page describes how the Awakening Bell Chrome extension handles data. The short version: it doesn’t.

What we collect

Nothing. The Awakening Bell extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, telemetry, analytics, crash reports, or any other information about you or your device.

What we send to remote servers

Nothing. The extension performs zero network requests. There is no backend, no analytics service, no error-reporting endpoint, and no telemetry of any kind. You can verify this by opening Chrome’s DevTools network tab while the popup is open — you will see no outbound traffic.

What we store locally

The extension saves your bell preferences on your own computer using Chrome’s chrome.storage.local API. This includes:

  • Your selected interval lengths for the periodic and random bell modes.
  • The list of scheduled reminder times you have added, including the bell type and time of day.

This data is stored only on your device. It is not synced to a Google account, not sent to any server, and not accessible to any other website or extension. You can clear it at any time by removing the extension or by clearing extension storage in Chrome’s settings.

Permissions the extension uses

  • alarms — to schedule bell sounds.
  • offscreen — to play audio from the background (a requirement of Chrome’s Manifest V3).
  • storage — to remember your preferences.

The extension does not request and does not have permission to read or modify any web page, see your browsing history, access your tabs, or use the network.

Children

The extension is appropriate for all ages. No data is collected from any user, including users under 13.

Changes to this policy
